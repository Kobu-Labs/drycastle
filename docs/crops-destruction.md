# Crops crushed by vehicles (#328)

Cosmetic only, like the crops themselves: nothing collides, slows or blocks a bullet. Pure rules `ShooterCore/Events/CropCrush.cs` and `CropCrushMask.cs` (tests `CropCrushTests`), server `NetServer.Harvester.cs` (`StepCrush`), client `NetClient.CropCrush.cs` (`StampCrush`, `OnCropCrush`, `FlushCrush`), map `MapLoader.Harvest.cs`.

- **Who.** Every ground vehicle (land or amphibious on land, not aircraft, not afloat), also the harvester's body, on a crop field (`CropFields`: grain, corn, potato, rapeseed; not `crop_bare`). Moving faster than 0.6 m/s with somebody at the wheel, or 2 m/s rolling free.
- **Footprint.** The body's width (95 % of `half_width` each side; vehicles.json has no wheel positions), stamped as a strip along the path of the vehicle's **leading end** (front bumper going forward, the rear reversing; `CropCrushRules.LeadM`: the strip's rounded end stops at the bumper), a sample every `CropCrushConfig.SampleM` = 2 m of travel (`SwathSampler`, not the reel's `SwathSampleM`). Which end leads is decided after 0.25 m of travel (`DirectionM`); turning round restarts the path.
- **One code, two stamps (#353).** `CropCrushMask` holds the masks, the cap and every vehicle's sampler, and does the whole decision (`Crushes`, ground only, field under the leading end, `Mark`). The server steps it with the real poses (authority); a client steps its own copy every frame, after `RenderCars`, with the pose it **draws** (own predicted car, interpolated others; speed from the drawn drive state, else the smoothed record speed; `driven` = a driver sits), so the track is under the car with no network delay. Server cells go through `CropCrushMask.Set`: a cell the client already stamped is skipped, a cell it never drew is added. No cell is ever un-crushed: a wrongly predicted cell stays flat until round reset (cosmetic, 1 m). Caps are counted per side (the client total can differ a little from the server's near the 120000 cap). Round reset clears the client mask, queue and paths (`CropCrushRestart`); a joiner's runs seed the mask. Headless clients (`ShotFx.Enabled == false`) stamp nothing. No per-frame allocation after a vehicle's first step (`Mark` fills a reused list).
- **Mask.** Per field a bit per 1 m cell of the field's bounding box (`CrushGrid`): at most 262144 cells per field (bigger fields are not tracked), 120000 crushed cells in all (`CropCrushConfig`). Ground driven over before adds nothing. Cleared at round reset, not kept across rounds.
- **Wire.** Event 238 `CropCrush`, every `CropCrushConfig.FlushS` = 0.1 s while cells are new (a fast car: tens of bytes per flush): the new cells as ascending grid indices, varint deltas, messages up to 200 bytes. A joiner gets each field's whole mask as alternating run lengths (clear, crushed, ...), same size limit. Nothing per plant, per vehicle or per frame.
- **Look.** The client lays the plants in the new cells flat once per frame (tilted over, squashed, not removed; corn too), per 48 m MultiMesh cell like the harvester's cut. A plant the reel cut stays stubble (cut wins over crush). A dark flat patch per crushed cell on the ground (one MultiMesh, 2048 and doubling) so the mark reads beyond the plants' 110 m range. A new round stands every plant up again (`MapLoader.CropReset`). `CropMarkGround` regrows its MultiMesh by doubling (2048, 4096, ...) and rewrites every instance then: a hitch is possible at those counts, not measured.

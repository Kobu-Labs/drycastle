# Scud event

Server `Net/Server/Scuds/ScudEventServer.cs` (start, tick, end), `ScudServer.cs` (the truck), `ScudLoading.cs` (the reload), `ScudBlastServer.cs` (the blast), `NetServer.Tow.cs` (the carts); client `Net/Client/Incidents/IntelClient.cs`, `IntelScreenView.cs`, `TacticalMap.cs` (the circle); pure rules `ShooterCore/Events/ScudEvent.cs` (tests `ScudEventTests`, `ScudReloadTests`, `ScudModelTests`). Machines: `Scud.Event`, `Scud.CartSlot`, `Scud.Launcher`, `Scud.ReloadKey` (`docs/state-machines.md`). Numbers: `scud.json` `event`, `reload`, `blast`. Design record: ADR 0034.

- **When.** `/event scud` (admin), one event at a time (the `EventBook`), refused while a truck from the last event still lives. No scheduler yet; `event.spawn_weight` is there for one. The admin's answer says where the truck really is (the only place that does); everybody else gets the standard announcement ("Suspicious activity detected, report to the base") and the intel entry.
- **The truck's place.** A random baked point of a `scud` zone (`PickEventSpawn`, `zone_points.json`), else a random player spawn point (open ground), at least `min_player_distance_m` (250) from every human, facing along the nearest road (any way when none is within 15 m), on real ground under the whole 3.1 x 11.9 m footprint with nothing solid in it grown by `spot_clearance_m` and no vehicle within a metre. The village map has no `scud` zone drawn: the fallback runs there until one is drawn and the map re-exported (below).
- **The intel entry.** "Top secret weapon located", military text, four risks, place = a **search area**: radius `search_radius_cells` (9) cells of the tactical map's grid (`TacticalMapMath.CellSize` of the world sheet, which the server works out the same way the clients do, `TacticalMapMath.Sheet`), centre = the truck plus a random vector (uniform over a disc of 97 % of the radius, `ScudSearch.Make`), so the truck is uniformly anywhere inside and the centre leaks nothing. The screen says the centre's grid reference and the radius in cells and draws a dashed circle with a small cross on its map; the tactical map (M) draws the same circle, labelled, while the event is on.
- **Nothing else shows the truck.** The client keeps launcher trucks off the minimap's and the tactical map's vehicle dots while an intel entry of kind `scud` with an area is on the board (`NetClient.ScudTruckHidden`); the entry goes the moment somebody boards. No enemy marker, no chat line and no toast says where. A modified client can still read the truck's position from the vehicle records and the entity snapshot (every vehicle is replicated to everyone, ADR 0025, and the truck's model is drawn wherever you stand within draw distance); that is accepted for a closed group.
- **Won / despawned.** Won when the first *human* (a bot never counts) is in a seat of the truck: `xp_reward` (400, `XpReason.EventWin`) to them, a toast to them ("You found the Scud truck"), a chat line to all, the intel entry and the circle withdrawn, the event slot free (`EventBook.End(.., Won)`). The truck is never despawned afterwards. Despawned (no reward, truck and carts removed): nobody boards it within `despawn_minutes` (20), the truck is destroyed unfound, `/event current cancel`, or a round ends. `/event current end` wins it for the caller.
- **Rocket carts.** At the start a loaded cart stands outside each bunker (`MapAreas.Shelters` level 0 rooms; the three of `bunkers.json`): the first of `ScudCartSpots` that is on real ground, clear of solids and of other vehicles; first along the doorway (found with rays through the physics world from the middle of the ground floor, since `bunkers.json` does not say where the door is), fanned either side of it, then all round; the cart's drawbar points away from the bunker so a vehicle reverses onto it from open ground, its rear end `cart_standoff_m` (3) off the bunker's footprint. When a bunker's cart is emptied (a truck took its rocket), destroyed or removed, the slot waits `cart_cooldown_seconds` (600), retires the emptied one if it is loose and puts a new loaded one (a blocked bunker tries again every 20 s). A cart that is only towed away loaded is still its slot's. When the truck is destroyed `RemoveRocketCarts()` removes every cart (a cart on a hook is unhitched first); a round ends them too (`returns: false`).
- **Reload from a cart.** An empty truck (rack down) with a loaded, live cart within 5 m of its hull (box to box, hitched or not): a player in the truck, or on foot within 4 m of it, holds interact for 1 s. Tap = get in or out as ever; the key's action then waits for the release, only while this offer stands (`ScudReloadKey`; the tow's unhitch key is another machine on another vehicle type). `Loading` is the first half of the 10 s reload: the truck braked, the cart still loaded; the truck or cart moving 1 m, or either being destroyed, cancels it with the cart untouched. At the half way point the rocket leaves the cart (empty from then on, lighter) and shows on the rack through `Reloading`. A shot loaded cart detonates with the Scud blast, credited to its shooter; loaded carts in a Scud blast's damage go up in turn (0.8 s plus the wave's travel), each once.
- **Persistence.** A new round ends the event with no reward: the world resets, and the truck, which never returns (`returns: false`, ADR 0033), is retired by the round reset like the harvester's machine; so are the carts. Nothing carries over.
- **Pipeline.** `scud` zone kind: `edits/zones.py` `KINDS`, `rules.yaml` `zones.scud` (2000 m2 per point, 10 to 200 points, 15 m from buildings, 10 m from water). Draw a `scud` zone in the editor's Zones tool (open ground, a field track, a road verge: room for a 12 m truck) and re-run the export for `zone_points.json` to get baked points; until then the fallback is used.

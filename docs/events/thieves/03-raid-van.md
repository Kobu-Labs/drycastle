# 03 Raid van

ADR 0021. The van drives in, lets the crew out, takes them back, drives off; one convoy wave of 1 to 3 vans makes the event.

## Facts
- `roads.json`: 191 nodes, 251 edges (from, to, 2D polyline, width, `highway`, `oneway`). Read as raw text by `MapLoader`; `RoadNetwork.Parse` (`Minimap/MinimapMath.cs`) and `RoadSurfaces.Parse` keep polylines only. No graph, no z (sample `Map.GroundHeight`).
- No marked entry points. Chunks cover x −750..2250, y −1750..2500; 14 nodes lie outside, so edges cross the edge.
- Scripted placement precedent: `VehicleThrow` (`Events/Ballistics.cs`). Hit target with its own pose history: the Bigfoot (`PoseRing`).
- Ramming: how a `ServerCar` hurts players and shoves cars (`VehicleContact.cs`, `PushOnDeath` in `NetServer.Vehicles.cs`).

## Do
- `RoadGraph` (pure, `godot/ShooterCore/Thieves/` or `Roads/`): parse nodes and edges, shortest path (Dijkstra, length), `EntryPoints(extent)` = where an edge crosses the map extent, `PointsAlong` for stop candidates. Oneway ignored.
- Stop choice: the candidate with the most stealable targets within `stop_radius_m` (80), not used this event. Until 04 to 07 there are no targets: count spawned cars, fall back to nearest to a player.
- `RaidVan : IWorldEntity`, new wire type. State machine: Arriving, Opening, Parked, Closing, Leaving, Destroyed (wreck lies, then ends). Follows the path at `speed`, yaw from the path, height from the ground.
- Pushes through: players in its box are rammed as by a car, vehicles are shoved aside. Never brakes. Honk: an fx event at random intervals and when a player is within `honk_near_m`.
- Hit count 300 (explosive weight in data), hittable while driving, rewound from its own pose ring. Solid for players and vehicles while parked.
- Crew: on Opening, members spawn at the side-door point, one every `crew_stagger_s`. Boarding: a member reaching the door ends (counted as boarded).
- Leave when: Loot loaded ≥ `capacity` (8; counter fed by 04), or `stay_seconds` (240) ran out; a dead crew does not send it off (it stands parked, solid, destroyable). Call the living crew back first; wait at most `board_wait_s`.
- Destroyed: Raid over, crew switches to Flee, hook `LootReturnAll()` for 04.
- `ThievesEvent`: one wave of vans (`raids.min_vans`..`max_vans` at random), launched `convoy_gap_seconds` apart on one route. Crew size by player count. After every van of the wave is gone: event won.
- Client: box van without the pack (a Kenney `van.glb` if simplest), door as a moving panel, wreck look, honk sound (reuse the closest existing id).

## Accept
- Tests: shortest path on a small graph, entry points on an extent, stop choice, van state machine (leave rules, destroyed while arriving), Raid sequencing to the win.
- `/event thieves`: the convoy comes and goes by itself with nobody near; the event ends won.

### API for 04 and on
- Server: `NetServer.Raids` (`RaidRoster`): `Raids` (each `Raid`: `.Van`, `.Gang`, `.Plan`, `.Over`, `.Boarded`, `.VanEntity`), `Active`, `UsedStops`, events `RaidOver`, `LootReturned(raid, n)`, `Honked`, `Launched`.
- Van counter (`RaidVan`): `Capacity` (8), `Load`, `Full`, `LoadLoot(n = 1)` (false unless the door is open - Opening, Parked, Boarding - and not full: 04 calls it when a Thief with Loot reaches the door; the Loot kind needs no count here), `LootReturnAll()` (empties, returns how many). Full van = leave (Parked goes to Boarding on its own).
- Door: `RaidVan.DoorPoint` (ground beside the van on `DoorSide`, a little ahead of centre; moves with the van, fixed while it stands), `DoorOpen` (0-1), `Solid`, `State`, `Position`, `Yaw`. The Raid sets `Gang.Post` to it when the door opens and `BoardAll` sends the crew to it. A Thief that reaches the door while called back ends as boarded (`GangMember.Board`/`Boarded`): 04 must unload what it carries into the van there (hook: `GangMember.Boarded` / `ThiefGang.Boarded`, `Carried` still set).
- Hook `LootReturnAll`: `NetServer.LootReturnAll(Raid, loaded)` in `NetServer.Thieves.Raid.cs` (called when a van is destroyed; `loaded` = what the counter held, already zeroed). 04 returns the Loot there. A van that drove off keeps its Loot (nothing to do).
- Stop choice: `NetServer.RaidTargetPoints()` returns the ground (x, z) of every stealable target; today the standing cars. 04 to 07 add their places there (tires: the cars' wheel spots; pets: gardens; dishes; poles). `RaidPlanner.Plan` counts those within `van.stop_radius_m` (80) of each candidate, drops candidates within `used_apart_m` (60) of a stop used this event, and picks the most; with none anywhere the nearest to a player.
- `RoadGraph` (pure; world ground axes: x east, `Y` = world z): `Parse(json, minWidthM)`, `EntryPoints(min, max)` (`RoadEntry`: border point, inside node, lead), `PointsAlong(step, min, max, inset)` (`RoadPoint`), `Nearest(p, maxM)`, `Dijkstra`, `ShortestPath(a, b)`, `RouteIn(entry, stop)`, `RouteOut(from, forward, entries, uTurnPenalty)`, `PointAt`, `YawAt`. `RoadPath`: `At(s)`, `YawAt(s)`, `Length`. Server: `RaidRoads` (cached, footpaths cut by `van.min_road_width_m`), `RaidExtent()` = the terrain's box (`MapFlightBounds`), `RaidGround(x, z)`. The SWAT bus can use all of it.
- Hit ids `0x73E0..0x73FF` (`RaidVanRules.FirstHitId`, two per van: front and rear capsule), `SpecialWeapons.Vehicle` for a van's run-over (attacker id = the van's hit id, the feed says "Car").

### Wire (protocol 44)
- World entity type 55 `raid van`: owner = door open in thousandths (bits 0-9) + bit 15 door on the left; state byte = `RaidVanState`; hp byte = bits 0-4 Loot loaded, bits 5-7 damage stage (0-7); position = centre on the ground, yaw, pitch = slope. `RaidVanRules.PackOwner/PackHp/DoorOf/LeftOf/LoadOf/StageOf`.
- Gang member state 8 `Board` (`GangMemberState`; the client runs it).
- Event kind 232 `ThievesFx` (`ThievesFxCodec`: u8 kind, point, u16 id; kind 0 Honk). 233-239 stay free for the Thieves.

### Decisions
- Van states: Arriving, Opening, Parked, **Boarding** (added, the ticket had it inside Closing: calls the crew back and waits `board_wait_seconds`), Closing, Leaving, Destroyed. Leave rules in `RaidVan.Tick`: Loot loaded >= capacity, stay clock (runs while Parked) ran out, or nobody afoot and none left to get out. The crew that the wait did not get flees when the van leaves (`Raid` calls `FleeAll`).
- `Raid` owns the crew spawn (one every `crew_stagger_seconds`, first as the door starts to open; order Enforcer, Gang boss, then Thieves and Enforcers alternating), `RaidRoster` the launch, hit routing (`MatchSim.Vans`), targets, pose ring and cleanup. A Raid is "over" when its van is destroyed or gone; the destroyed wreck lies `wreck_seconds` (25) and is solid.
- Hit volume: the weapon paths know capsules only, so the van is two prone player capsules at scale 2.2 (radius 1.23 m, 2.46 m high), 0.9 m ahead and behind its centre, headshot = body hit. Rewound like the Bigfoot from one `PoseRing` per van (the capsules are derived from the centre pose). One explosion reaches both capsules: the same blast of the same attacker in the same tick counts once (`RaidVan.Hit`). Weights in `van.hit`: bullet 1, headshot 1, explosive 25 (12 rockets), vehicle weapon 6; ramming counts nothing. Turrets and killstreak weapons shoot vans; bots do not (not in their lists).
- Shooting a van starts the Raid's aggro against the shooter (not in the design text: shooting the van is interfering).
- Ramming: `RaidContacts` sweeps the van's box over the tick, `RunOver.Damage` of vehicles.json at its speed and `van.ram_mass_scale` (3), roadkill thrown ahead, same per-victim cooldown as a car; riders in vehicles skipped. Gang members of other Raids are run over dead; its own crew is not. Vehicles: `ShoveCarsByVans` in `ResolveVehicleContacts`, `VehicleContact` bump with a van mass of 1e7 (a car is moved out and loses speed, takes crash damage by the loss). The van is solid for vehicles whenever it exists (also parked).
- Solid for players: a `StaticBody3D` on `VehicleSolid` (server `PlaceVanSolids`, client `PlaceVanSolid` from the state's own numbers, as `VehicleCollider` for cars) while the state is Opening, Parked, Boarding, Closing or Destroyed. A player inside the box when it stops is put out through the nearest side (`RaidVanRules.PushOut`).
- Stop and entry: the map edge is the terrain's box (`MapFlightBounds`, the same rectangle the helicopter flies in; the roads run far outside it). An entry is the last out-to-in crossing of an edge whose rest stays inside. Roads narrower than 3 m are cut (footpaths; tracks of 3 m stay: the village graph has one component). Up to 60 reachable stop candidates are tried against the navigation mesh (the crew must be able to step out: `AcceptRaidStop` snaps the door point, either side). Way out: shortest to the nearest entry, driving on preferred (`u_turn_penalty_m` 150).
- Fallback (no graph usable, no entry, no reachable stop): parked at a road point 25+ m from the admin (else any human, else the map's middle), logged "(fallback)". `/event thieves` refuses only with no roads at all or no navigation mesh. No admin needed afterwards: nothing waits for a player.
- Event: first van on the next tick, each next `convoy_gap_seconds` (3) after the one before, the same route for all, a stop of each own (a van with none is dropped, `ThievesEvent.Shrink`). Natural win leaves fleeing crews to run to the edge; admin `end` and `cancel` and a round reset remove everything (vans, crews, boxes, used stops). Crew size: +1 member per `players_per_extra` (2) humans beyond the first, Thieves up to +3, then Enforcers up to +1.
- Names: `speed_mps`, `board_wait_seconds` etc. follow the file's `_seconds` style (the ticket said `speed`, `board_wait_s`).
- Client: box van of boxes and cylinders (body, cab, dark windscreen, 4 wheels that turn, side door as a sliding panel over a dark opening), paint dims per damage stage, wreck = charred and canted, door hanging; vehicle blast when it is seen destroyed. Honk plays `helper.warning` (the closest id: a short low buzz; it is a UI sound with no range, so the client fades it by distance up to `van.view.honk_hear_m`). Nothing loaded from assets-private.

## Fixed routes
Change after the ticket (owner): vans enter and leave strictly along two fixed routes, not a random map-edge road. Code: pure `godot/ShooterCore/Thieves/RaidRoutes.cs` (`RaidRoutes.Resolve`, `Order`, `Plan`; `ResolvedRoute`), `RoadGraph.DijkstraFrom` / `RouteBetween` (place to place), `RoadPath.Slice` / `Reversed` / `Then` / `DistanceTo`, `RaidPlan.Out` and `RouteName`; server `LaunchRaid` (`RaidRouteList`, `RaidRouteRoads`, `RaidParkCentre`). Tests `RaidRoutesTests.cs`. No wire change.
- Data: `raids.routes` (name + waypoints [x, z], the map's local ground metres = roads.json axes with y flipped), `route_pick` "random", `route_snap_max_m` 30, `route_min_road_width_m` 0, `park_near_church_m` 100, `park_centre` [x, z] (overrides the church), `route_stop_search_m` 40. The two routes are the VILLAGE map's: sketch points A B C (route 1) and D E F G H (route 2) of the shared `~/.local/state/drycastle/sketch.json` (was `out/sketch.json`), converted with the map origin (`origin_epsg5514` of parcels.json / frame.json): x = X - ox, z = -(Y - oy).
- A Raid picks a route at random (plain, seeded). The van appears at the first waypoint (snapped to the road graph), drives the shortest road path between consecutive waypoints (each snapped to the nearest road point within `route_snap_max_m`), then on from the last waypoint, never turning back over it, to a stop and parks. Leaving: the same roads backwards to the first waypoint, then gone (the van turns on the spot at the stop: no reverse gear).
- Stop: road points (`stop_step_m` spacing) within `park_near_church_m` of the church doorway (church.json via `MapChurch`, or `park_centre`), reachable from the route's end, not within `used_apart_m` of a stop of this event, passing the door-point / navmesh check; one at random, those on the main road (the shortest road path from the route's end to the church) first, else any in range, else the nearest working spot to the church anywhere. None, or no church and no `park_centre`: the end of the route (the exact spot, else the nearest working spot back along it within `route_stop_search_m`; a spot within `used_apart_m` of another van's moves back along the route until it is that far short). No route stop works: next route; none: the old automatic way (kept, with its tests).
- A route that cannot be resolved (a waypoint farther than `route_snap_max_m` from the roads, no road path between two waypoints, last waypoint not on a van road) is skipped with one server log line; no usable route: the old automatic behaviour.
- Route legs may use roads of any width (`route_min_road_width_m` 0): route 2's G to H is a 1.5 m footpath (E-0016); with the van's 3 m minimum that leg detours 523 m out and back over the track. From the route's end to the stop the van's own `van.min_road_width_m` applies.

# Harvester event (#331)

Server `NetServer.Harvester.cs`, client `NetClient.Harvester.cs`, pure rules `ShooterCore/Events/Harvester*.cs`, `CropFields.cs`, `CropCrush.cs` (tests `HarvesterTests`, `CropCrushTests`). Machines: `Harvester.Ai`, `Hay.Bale`, `Harvester.Corpse` (`docs/state-machines.md`). Vehicles also flatten crops: `docs/crops-destruction.md`.

- **When.** One at a time (the `EventBook`). After one is done (left the field, driver killed, machine destroyed, cancelled) no new one for 12 min (`HarvesterConfig.CooldownS`, a `Countdown`). Auto spawn: 10 min after start or round, then a roll every 60 s with 12 % while a human plays and no other event runs; the field must have a human within 350 m of its outline. `/event harvester` (admin) ignores the cooldown, refuses while one is out, answers with field id and x,y,z in map metres (x east, y up, z south).
- **Fields.** The ground patches of `patches.json` (id, type, ring) whose type is `crop_*` except `crop_bare` (`MapLoader.PatchPaints`, `CropFields`). The wire id of a field is its index in that list. No export change.
- **Machine.** vehicles.json `harvester` (`pack:synty-farm/SM_Veh_Harvester_01`, box without the pack): one hidden `ServerCar` reused for every job. `run_over` false: the body kills nobody, only the reel does. Driver: a seated bot (`Client.HarvesterDriver`), shot dead it stays seated (ADR 0026) and anyone takes the seat once it rests (the kick-out is #330). A player may drive any harvester (stolen, `/spawn`ed): cut, catch and bales work for whoever sits at the wheel.
- **Reel.** `HarvesterConfig.Reel` (`ReelBox`, machine space): 3.66 m ahead of the origin, 2.5 m to each side, 0.9 m fore and aft, -0.3..2.2 m high (the model's reel is 4.85 m wide, 0.68 m radius; test `Only_the_reel_box_catches...`). Only it cuts and catches.
- **Drive.** `FieldPlanner`: lanes along the longest edge (header 4.8 m, 0.3 m overlap), driven in an order that skips lanes: the next pass is the nearest lane at least `ceil(2R / 4.5)` = 4 lanes away, so every turn is a half circle of radius >= `TurnRadiusM` (7 m, vehicles.json `turn_radius`); the lanes in between are cut on the way back. The last lanes (none 4 away left) get a bulb turn of three arcs of R (`AddBulb`). Way out along the inset edge. `HarvesterBrain` steers with pure pursuit (`HarvesterPilot`, steer 1 = tightest radius).
- **Cut.** Cosmetic, the real path: while somebody drives a harvester forward over a crop field the server samples the reel's middle every 2 m (`SwathSampler`), keeps a stretch when at least 30 % of its swath is new ground (`FieldSwath.TryAdd`: re-driven ground adds nothing) and sends the kept stretches twice a second (`EventKind.HarvesterCut`: field, width, polyline of quarter-metre points, first absolute then byte deltas, <= 100 points). Clients squash the crop along them (`MapLoader.CropCut`). A joiner gets each field's stretches (history bounded: 6000 points per field, 40000 in all, messages <= 207 bytes). `HarvesterState` (phase, field, pass, metres, twice a second) is now only the AI's progress and the signal that it drove off.
- **Bales it drops.** On `crop_grain` and `crop_rapeseed` one round bale per 60 m of new cut, at most 24 per field, where the unloader was (`DroppedBaleBehindM`, `DroppedBaleLeftM`). Not sent: server and clients derive them from the same kept stretches (`FieldSwath.Bales`, test `A_client_replaying...`). 10 min after the last cut on a field they fade out for all (`DroppedBaleFadeS`: the server counts it, a client counts from the `AgeS` it was told); a round reset removes them. They stay ignitable like the field's own (Fire), per bale by position (`FxMsg` id with both high bits); burnt ones go to a joiner as effects. No collision.
- **Kills.** Run in front of the reel (`SpecialWeapons.Harvester`, "Harvester reel"): credited to the driver, so the kill feed and score count them. The body spins round the reel for 3.6 s (`DrawSeconds`: the server carries the corpse to the reel's axis, 3.66 m ahead and 0.81 m up, `HarvesterCatch`), spends 2.5 s inside (`ChewSeconds`, unchanged), and then ONE bale (a corpse owned by 60002, Items on it, the victim's body inside) is thrown from the unloader. The victim's own corpse is removed when it goes inside; the bale is the only object after that. The body's run-over is off (default: reel only).
- **The thrown bale.** Spawned at the unloader (behind the machine, 1.6 m to the left, `UnloaderHeightM` 1.6 m up), launched backwards 7 m/s, 2.5 m/s to the left and 5 m/s up on top of the machine's velocity (`HarvesterCatch.BaleVelocity`, `Corpse.Launch(rolls: true)`). The server flies it like any thrown corpse (`StepCorpses`); on landing it keeps 70 % of its horizontal speed and rolls with friction (`Corpse.Flight` state `Rolling`: 3.5 m/s2, stops under 0.4 m/s or against a wall, flies on if the ground drops away, floats in water). Its `HayBale.Position` follows it (`FollowMadeBales`), so loot reach (from the server position), fire and scorch use where it is. In the snapshot its pitch is 1 while it flies and 0.25 while it rolls. Only a player's bale flies; the crop bales stay where they fall.
- **Corpse bales** never expire: no lifetime, not counted by the corpse cap, still there when their items are taken. Only fire (ash), a round reset, or the safety cap `CorpseBook.MaxBales` = 60 (the oldest goes) removes one.
- **Catch, seen and heard.** Fx `Drawn` (reel grabs: whirr and engine strain `harvester.reel_in`, 3.7 s; the body, tumbling, is gathered onto an orbit of 0.95 m round the reel's axis in the first 0.45 s, then spins round it at the reel's speed `ReelSpin` 3.4 rad/s, about two turns in 3.6 s, and in the last quarter is drawn back into the machine and shrinks; the machine rumbles for the next 6.1 s), `Swallowed` (muffled `harvester.chew`), `BaleOut` (chute `harvester.eject` and `harvester.pop`, the bale grows in over 0.25 s and the server throws it: the client smooths the 30 Hz positions, tumbles it in the air, turns it by the distance it rolls and lets it stop; the victim's body is drawn small at its centre while it moves and is inside at rest). The three effects carry the victim's player id: the dead avatar stays hidden through all of it (the victim's corpse is gone after `Swallowed`). No death camera change: a 3 s respawn comes before the bale is out. Sounds of the machine the local player rides (reel, chew, eject, pop) play at the listener, not at a point on it (`NetClient.RidesCar`, `docs/standards/car-design.md` section 9, #352); for everybody else reel and chew follow the moving machine. `harvester.pop` is also played for a crop bale when its message is live (`SwathCodec` replay flag, not for a joiner's history). The pop is the private clip `assets-private/audio/harvester/pop.ogg` when it is installed, else `harvester_pop.ogg` of the repo (sounds.json `first_found`). Other sounds are synthesised by `scripts/make_harvester_sounds.py`. Protocol 65.
- **Wheels, cockpit.** The wheels roll with the ground speed (radius 0.733 m front, 0.554 m rear) and the front pair steers (`RollHarvesterWheels`). The driver has a cockpit view (`CockpitRig.Specs["harvester"]`: eye over the seat, a turning wheel and hands; the model's own wheel and glass are hidden).
- **Fire.** Any hitscan bullet or pellet that passes a bale lights it (also the field's `art_hay_bale*`, `MapLoader.CropBales`); 6 s, players within 2.6 m burn (`BurnSet.HazardSource`, `BurnCredit`: nobody is credited); then ash. Not rockets or blasts yet.

## 1. Verdict

**Fit with changes.** The static world is a good fit: trees, fences and terrain are merged into chunk nodes, lamps and clutter are MultiMeshes, and the colour material is shared. Node-per-entity is a good fit for the things that are really entities (16 characters, 29 vehicles, the 12 pooled lights, a damaged pane's overlay). The fit breaks in two places. (a) Effects are built from scene nodes per event: `RocketFx.Explode` (`godot/shooter/RocketFx.cs:127-163`) is not pooled and not distance-gated, and a gunship cannon fires one blast per round. (b) The map is one draw per visual node: 1,959 nodes on 0.10.0 against 998 in the audit. The binding constraint is the render thread's draw submission (CPU), not triangles: the visible triangle load is about 1 M at median and 2 M at p90 (manifest, chunk-level), which a 2016 mid-range GPU can push at 60 fps, while the visible draw count reaches roughly 600 to 1,000 for the map alone (section 3, F-01). The design stops fitting at about 1,500 to 2,000 draws per frame (render thread about 8 to 30 ms at an assumed 5 to 15 µs per draw, UNVERIFIED). That is reached at the p90 view with 16 remote characters, a few corpses, and two gunship gunners firing. Normal play on the assumed floor is inside the budget.

**Assumed floor.** 1080p, VSync on (60 fps), a GTX 1060 6 GB / RX 580 class GPU, a 4-core 8-thread CPU at about 3 GHz (2017 desktop). Intel UHD 630 is the stress case. Basis: `docs/pipeline.md:62` says the village profile is tuned "for 16-64 players on weak GPUs", and `docs/perf-audit.md:278` says the target is weak GPUs; neither gives a number. The floor is OPINION.

## 2. What is healthy

- **One shared map material.** `MapLoader.cs:410-425` (`FlatMaterial`, `#171`). Fixes step 1 of T2-20: `FixMaterials` (`MapLoader.cs:413`) no longer builds a `StandardMaterial3D` per node. CONFIRMED.
- **Clutter is instanced with visibility ranges.** One MultiMesh per (chunk, kind, 48 m cell) with at least 16 instances, small groups merged into one mesh per cell (`MapLoader.Clutter.cs:88-102`). Each node is shadowless, flat and has a range (`MapLoader.Clutter.cs:139-142`). Constants are in `ClutterData.cs:85-97` (`CellM` 48, `MultiMeshMin` 16, `DefaultRangeM` 70, `FadeMarginM` 8). Meadow tiles also have ranges (`MapLoader.Meadow.cs:222`, `:296-298`, `:321-323`). The clutter build yields once per chunk (`MapLoader.cs:141`), so load does not hitch on it. Measured from `clutter.json` (CONFIRMED arithmetic): median 20 visible instances per sampled ground position, p90 about 3,000, max about 4,000; visible triangles median 360, p90 47,000, max 62,000 (mesh tri counts from the GLB, instance counts within each kind's range plus one cell).
- **Trees are not nodes.** Visual trees are part of the chunk GLBs (58,370 trees, about 26 triangles each: 1,544,350 props triangles / 58,370). Tree colliders are one body per chunk (`MapLoader.Trees.cs:15-18`, `:60`), though their shape count is a physics issue (E).
- **Lamps are MultiMeshes.** Poles, heads and glow are one MultiMesh each (`StreetLamps.cs:113`, `:122`, `:151-156`, `:160-166`). The glow has `VisibilityRangeEnd` 260 m (`StreetLamps.cs:28`, `:154-155`). The light pool is 12 shadowless omni lights (`StreetLamps.cs:18`, `:185`), refreshed every 0.2 s (`:27`, `:76-81`), not every frame. Headless: no visuals, no light pool, no `_Process` (`StreetLamps.cs:57`, `:66`). This closes T3-021.
- **Effect pools that work.** Tracers (32) and sparks (16) are pooled and reused round-robin (`ShotFx.cs:17-18`, `:48-75`, `:122-130`). Shots beyond 300 m or from outside `EffectRange` are skipped (`ShotFx.cs:78-82`, `:95`, `:137`). Impact emitters are pooled per size (`Net/NetClient.Impacts.cs:19-21`, `:56-76`, `:98-120`), the ring and dust pools too. `ParticleFx.cs:6-39` documents and avoids a real engine trap (an emitter that has not emitted draws stale instance memory) and hides idle emitters.
- **Client nodes are freed on leave.** `Owned` is one node per client (`Net/NetClient.cs:27-33`); `Teardown` and `FreeOwned` free it (`Net/NetClient.cs:143-172`). This closes T2-17 (#169, commit `55c4390`).
- **Muzzle flash light is hidden with its parent.** The flash node and its `OmniLight3D` are invisible until a shot (`MuzzleFlash.cs:17-29`, `:36-43`), so idle weapons cost no light.
- **Bullet holes are a 64-decal ring** that fades out (`BulletHoles.cs:11`, `:12`, `:17`, `:140-149`).
- **Character tufts** are hidden beyond 45 m and do not cast shadows (`CharacterView.Camo.cs:22`, `:323-324`). Arm IK runs only while armed (`CharacterFx.cs:241-244` sets `_arms.Active`; `SkeletonModifier3D` skips inactive modifiers).
- **Glass.** A pane gets an overlay only once it has been hit (`Net/NetClient.Glass.cs:59-65`, `GlassFx.cs:60-75`), and the overlay is shadowless (`GlassFx.cs:73`). ADR 0008's pane nodes are in the vehicle GLBs and are a bounded count per vehicle (24 at most, `ADR 0008`).
- **Renderer features that are not used.** No SSAO, SSIL, SDFGI, SSR, volumetric fog or glow is set in the shooter (grep of `godot/shooter` and `godot/MapLoader`; `DayNightCycle.cs:55-61` builds the only `Environment`). That keeps a renderer switch cheap (F-06).
- **Shooter view and cull distances.** Camera FOV 90 (`Settings.cs:28`, `Player.cs:16`), far 2,000 m, which is also what the fog covers (below).
- **HUD and minimap are change-guarded.** `Hud.cs:1-12` (setters compare before writing), the scoreboard redraws only when dirty (`Hud.cs:618`). The tactical map's sheet redraws only on layout (`TacticalMap.cs:217`); only its marker layer redraws each frame (`TacticalMap.cs:170`, cheap).
- **Map load is stepped.** One step per chunk with a frame between steps (`Shooter.cs:205`, `:244`).
- **Lighting budget is capped.** Car headlamps: two spot beams for at most two vehicles (`CarLights.cs:9`, `:16`, `:25`). Explosion and muzzle lights are short-lived (F-02).
- **Server is headless and cheap on visuals.** `CollisionOnly` for the map (`Shooter.cs:329`) and lamps (`StreetLamps.cs:57`) closes most of T2-26.

## 3. Findings

Audit status is given for each. "New" means not in `docs/perf-audit.md`.

**F-01. Map draws: one draw per visual node, 1,959 nodes on the 0.10.0 map.** Mechanism: each `MeshInstance3D` is one draw call and nothing is batched by the engine. The shooter keeps the chunk GLB's node structure (`MapLoader.cs:330-345` generates the scene and adds it under one chunk node; the per-node loop follows it). Cost: visible chunks at a random view (`out/village/map/manifest.json`, 400 random ground positions, a 120° horizontal wedge, far 2,000 m, chunk-level, CONFIRMED arithmetic): median 43 chunks, p90 76. Visible map draws = 1,959 × (chunks in view / 145) ≈ 580 median and 1,025 at p90. At a 1,300 m far plane: median 30 chunks and p90 44, so about 405 and 595 draws. Fix: one merged `ArrayMesh` per (chunk, material) for static parcel and terrain nodes, keeping per-parcel nodes only where `ReplaceParcel` needs them (the viewer, `godot/viewer/Viewer.cs:205`, `:258`; see the "Replacement scene" entry in `GLOSSARY.md` and the comment at `godot/MapLoader/MapLoader.cs:27`). Note that `ReplaceParcel` is at `MapLoader.cs:563` and `RestoreParcel` at `:584`. Effort M. Confidence: node count CONFIRMED; draw cost UNVERIFIED. Audit: T2-20 step 2 (open); T2-22 partly. The 0.10.0 numbers are new.

**F-02. Explosions are built per blast from fresh nodes, with no pool and no gate (T2-16, open).** Mechanism: `RocketFx.Explode` (`RocketFx.cs:127-163`) creates a root, three `MeshInstance3D`s (`:137-139`), an `OmniLight3D` (`:151`), four `CpuParticles3D` emitters (`:157-160`, 74 particles), four tweens (`:141-154`), and a `SceneTreeTimer` closure (`:162`, via `ShotFx.cs:143`). It has no distance check. Rate: every gunship splash emits a blast (`Net/NetServer.Aircraft.cs:207`), and the coalesced blast message carries up to 8 (`ShooterCore/Aircraft/GunshipFx.cs:21`). The cannons fire every 0.15 s (gun25, `killstreaks.json` line 44; cannon, line 35) and 0.2 s (gun40, line 46), so one cannon gunner emits 5 to 6.7 blasts per second. Live explosions at once: rate × 2.2 s lifetime ≈ 11 to 15 per gunner. Per gunner that is about 14 objects × 15 = 210 nodes, about 7 draws × 15 = 105 draws, and about 1,100 CPU particles simulated (CONFIRMED arithmetic; per-particle and per-draw cost UNVERIFIED). Lights stay at about 2 to 3 per gunner because each light lives 0.35 s (`RocketFx.cs:151-154`). Fix: a pool of six explosion kits restarted in place (the `ShotFx` pattern), a per-tick coalesce into one kit at the tick's largest radius, a distance gate with `EffectCull` (`ShooterCore/EffectCull.cs:10-25`), and ring and fade driven by the kit's `_Process`. Effort M. Confidence: the pattern and rate CONFIRMED; frame cost UNVERIFIED. Audit: T2-16 (open, rate now quantified; the cannon interval is the new part).

**F-03. Residual churn in the effects that are still per event (T2-15, partly fixed).** Fixed by `55c4390`: tracers, sparks, impacts pooled and gated. Residual: (a) each shot creates a `Tween` and a callback closure (`ShotFx.cs:116-119`), about 160 per second at 16 players firing 10 shots/s (the audit's rate, UNVERIFIED); (b) each water splash creates a `CpuParticles3D` and a `SceneTreeTimer` (`WaterFx.cs:25-34`), gated only for droplets (`WaterFx.cs:22`), rate set by water events. Both are small. Fix: pool the splash emitter (two or three, the `ShotFx` pattern) and the tracer's tween into the pool slot (the slot already has `_tracerTweens`, `ShotFx.cs:27`). Effort S. Confidence CONFIRMED (structure), cost UNVERIFIED. Audit: T2-15 partly closed.

**F-04. Sun shadows cover all map casters with no distance and no per-node opt-out (T2-21, open; cost re-priced).** Mechanism: the sun has `ShadowEnabled = true` (`DayNightCycle.cs:39`, set per frame at `:107` when `SunEnergy > 0.01`). The shooter sets no `DirectionalShadowMaxDistance` (the viewer sets 250 m, `viewer/Viewer.cs:160`). No `CastShadow` change is made in `MapLoader.cs`, so terrain, detail patches and props cast. Cost: each cascade redraws every caster in its frustum. Engine defaults are UNVERIFIED (I believe 4 cascades and a 100 m max distance in Godot 4). Triangles are modest: the average chunk is 22,442 triangles over 62,500 m², so a 100 m radius is about 0.4 to 1 triangle per m², giving roughly 0.1 to 0.4 M triangles across cascades. The draw count is the bigger cost: about 100 to 250 extra draws per frame (UNVERIFIED). The audit's "hot by day" is overstated for triangles and right for draws. Fix: `CastShadow = Off` on terrain, detail and ground-level props at load; an explicit `DirectionalShadowMaxDistance` of 80 to 120 m; keep characters and vehicles casting. Effort S. Confidence CONFIRMED (absence), cost UNVERIFIED.

**F-05. MSAA 4x is the default (T2-23, open).** `Settings.cs:20` sets `Msaa = 2`, which `MsaaNames` (`Settings.cs:39`) reads as 4x; `Settings.cs:129` maps it to `Msaa4X`. The water shader reads the depth buffer (`water_surface.gdshader:11`, `:25`) and blends (`:4`), which forces a resolve on the MSAA target. Cost: four samples per pixel of colour and depth at 1080p, roughly 4× the MSAA memory traffic (UNVERIFIED). Fix: default 2x (`Settings.cs:20` to 1), with a render-scale setting. Effort S. Confidence CONFIRMED (setting), cost UNVERIFIED.

**F-06. Forward+ is the default and nothing in the shooter needs it (T3-061, open).** `project.godot` (28 lines) has no `rendering_method` key, so Forward+ applies. The shooter uses no Forward+-only feature (see section 2). Forward+ is clustered lights: the scene has 2 directional lights, 12 pool lights (`StreetLamps.cs:18`), up to 4 spot beams (`CarLights.cs:16`), and per-event omni lights (`RocketFx.cs:151`, `FlakFx.cs:53`, `VehicleFx.cs:120`), plus one per muzzle flash (`MuzzleFlash.cs:29`) while flashing. Whether zero-energy pool lights still take cluster work is UNVERIFIED. Options: Mobile is one line in `project.godot` and an M effort to validate (screen-texture and depth-texture shaders, `Decal`s in `BulletHoles.cs` and `CharacterView.Camo.cs:376`, water). Compatibility is not a fit: the shooter depends on `Decal` and screen-texture shaders, and the launcher's use of `gl_compatibility` (`launcher/project.godot:27`) is a different problem. Confidence: setting CONFIRMED; benefit UNVERIFIED. Audit: T3-061 (open).

**F-07. Clutter node count is higher than the code comment says (new).** `ClutterData.cs:88-89` says "1,715 nodes instead of 2,809" for the village. Grouping `clutter.json` by the code's rule (per chunk and kind and 48 m cell; MultiMesh at 16 or more, else one merged node per chunk and cell) gives 2,189 MultiMesh nodes plus 2,287 merged nodes, 4,476 in all. The comment predates 0.10.0 (`clutter.json` is dated 01:11 today; `pipeline.md:426` says "23 tiny meshes" for 0.10.0). Visible clutter nodes per view: median 42, p90 93, max 123 (CONFIRMED arithmetic for the grouping; the true count is UNVERIFIED until the scene is built). Cost: the scene tree holds about 5,500 renderable nodes (4,476 clutter plus 1,959 map plus lamps); culling is BVH-based so the per-frame cost is small (UNVERIFIED). Fix: none now; measure the node count (`Performance.ObjectNodeCount`) and, if it is above about 3,000, merge clutter per chunk and kind and cell, or raise `MultiMeshMin`. Effort S to M. Confidence UNVERIFIED for the count.

**F-08. First-use hitches: material and shader variants, and lazy loads (T2-18, open; extended).** Mechanism: Godot compiles a pipeline for each new material variant on its first draw. The shooter creates 38 `StandardMaterial3D` sites and 5 `ShaderMaterial` sites (grep), and some are built per event on first use (`RocketFx.cs:53-70`, `Net/NetClient.Impacts.cs:58`, `:82`, `:134`). Shaders are loaded lazily (`CharacterView.Camo.cs:121`, `:246`, `GunshipOverlay.cs:25`, `MissilePovOverlay.cs:35`). First-use hitch sizes are UNVERIFIED. Fix: in the loading steps, draw one hidden instance of each material and shader variant (the first shot, the first explosion, the first remote character, the first vehicle, the first gunship blast). Effort S to M. Confidence pattern OPINION (standard Godot 4 behaviour), size UNVERIFIED. Audit: T2-18 (open); the variant part is new.

**F-09. Characters and corpses: materials per character, and corpses scale with 16 players (T2-24, partly; T3-029, partly).** Per character: three `StandardMaterial3D` (`CharacterView.Camo.cs:123-125`), a suit `ShaderMaterial` (`:142`), a tuft `ShaderMaterial` (`:308`) and a face `Decal` (`:376`). Corpses are full `CharacterView`s (`Net/NetClient.Corpses.cs:51-57`), at most 16 (`ShooterCore/Corpses/Corpses.cs:13`) and each lasting 120 s (`:11`). Draw count per character is about 20 to 25 (audit, UNVERIFIED): 16 players plus 16 corpses is about 700 draws if all are visible, which is the largest single item in the budget. Arm IK runs for every armed remote at any distance (`ArmsReadyPose.cs:82`, active from `CharacterFx.cs:244`). Fix: cache materials by look (one set per look, not per character), `CastShadow = Off` on the body except the head, and skip skeleton and animation work for corpses beyond about 60 m (the corpse is static). Effort S to M. Confidence CONFIRMED (structure), draw count UNVERIFIED. Audit: T2-24, T3-029 (both open, and the corpse cap of 16 is new to this analysis).

**F-10. Minimap redraws every frame (T3-034, open).** `Minimap.cs:103-108` calls `QueueRedraw` each frame, and `_Draw` (`:110-159`) draws the terrain texture, every road in view (`DrawRoads`, `:162-182`), vehicle squares, pings and the north marker. The player moves, so the view changes while moving, but it does not change at rest; at the 120 fps focused cap (`Shooter.cs:62`, `:86`) that is 120 redraws per second for nothing when idle. Cost: visible roads per frame not counted (UNVERIFIED); each redraw re-tessellates every road polyline. Fix: a dirty key on quantised player position, yaw, marker list version and texture side; redraw when it changes. Effort S. Confidence CONFIRMED (structure), cost UNVERIFIED.

**F-11. Day and night writes every frame (T3-035, open; low value).** `DayNightCycle.cs:96-127` writes about 20 properties and 8 shader parameters each frame. Each is a native call with a small value; the total is likely tens of microseconds (UNVERIFIED). Fix: skip when the hour, quantised to 0.01 h, has not moved. Effort S. Confidence OPINION that it is worth little.

**F-12. The first-person viewmodel casts shadows (T3-054, open).** `WeaponViewmodel.cs:12-23` adds weapon models with no `CastShadow` setting, and `WeaponFx.Make` does not set one. The viewmodel is in the main world, so it is in the first cascade, and no one sees its shadow. Fix: `CastShadow = Off` on the viewmodel's meshes. Effort S. Confidence CONFIRMED (absence).

**F-13. Explosion particles are alpha-blended at 0.99 (T3-051, open).** `RocketFx.cs:30` sets `Color(c.R, c.G, c.B, 0.99f)`, so every explosion particle is in the transparent pass with depth writes and no early-z. Fix: opaque for fire and sparks (additive for glow), alpha for smoke only. Effort S. Confidence CONFIRMED; cost UNVERIFIED (overdraw of about 1,100 particles per gunner, F-02).

**F-14. Water is alpha-blended and reads depth (T3-057, open).** `water_surface.gdshader:4` (`blend_mix`), `:11` and `:25` (depth read, which needs a depth copy when water is visible). Fix: opaque water with a per-vertex shore factor, or accept the cost. Effort M. Confidence CONFIRMED; cost UNVERIFIED.

**F-15. Screen-copy overlays are conditional (T3-052, open, low).** `underwater.gdshader:4` and `gunship_thermal.gdshader:5` use `hint_screen_texture` with mipmaps. The underwater overlay is not drawn when `amount` is 0 (`underwater.gdshader:3`). The thermal overlay is only on while the gunship view is up. Cost only in those states. Effort S. Confidence CONFIRMED (conditional).

**F-16. Camouflage and crack shaders do per-fragment loops (T3-056, open, low).** `camo_suit.gdshader:20-68` runs 3 to 5 value-noise calls per fragment (each eight hashes), on up to 16 characters near the camera; `glass_crack.gdshader:31`, `:47-59` run a 12-edge loop and a 9- and 25-cell loop per fragment, but only on damaged panes. The cost scales with on-screen character and pane area, which is small (UNVERIFIED). Effort M to bake. Confidence CONFIRMED (structure), low priority.

**F-17. Lamp glow and poles are drawn in daylight (T3-050, open, trivial).** The glow MultiMesh is drawn at alpha 0 by day (`StreetLamps.cs:148`), and poles cast shadows (`:113`, no `CastShadow` set). That is one draw each. Fix: `Visible = level > 0` and `CastShadow = Off` on poles. Effort S. Confidence CONFIRMED.

**F-18. Map load and the draw budget have no measured baseline (new).** No frame-time or draw-count figure exists in the repo for any of the above (the audit's own "needs measurement" list, `docs/perf-audit.md:353-390`, says the same). Measurement plan in section 5.

Items already fixed and not repeated: T2-17 (`55c4390`), T2-20 step 1 (`#171`), T3-053 (impact pools per size, `Impacts.cs:19`), T3-021 and part of T2-26 (`StreetLamps.cs:57`, `:66`; `Shooter.cs:329`), and the per-map terrain texture cache of T2-19 (`#170`, `MapBase.cs:21-23`; the first rasterise still runs on the main thread).

## 5. Needs measurement

Nothing below was run. The order is the cheapest test first.

1. **Draw calls and objects per frame at fixed views.** Godot monitors `Performance.RenderTotalDrawCallsInFrame`, `RenderTotalObjectsInFrame` and `RenderTotalPrimitivesInFrame`, plus `ObjectNodeCount`. Views: a spawn at ground level (the median case), the p90 wedge, the same view with 16 characters, and the same view with a gunship firing one cannon. Answers F-01, F-02, F-07, F-09. Why not static: the culled count depends on frustum, visibility range and LOD in the engine.
2. **Render-thread time.** Godot's visual profiler (render thread) at the same views, and `TIME_PROCESS` and `TIME_PHYSICS_PROCESS` for the main thread. Answers whether the budget is CPU (draws) or GPU (fill). Why not static: the per-draw cost is a driver and engine property.
3. **GPU frame time with MSAA off, 2x and 4x and with sun shadows on and off, Forward+ and Mobile** (`Settings.cs:20`, `project.godot`). Answers F-04, F-05, F-06. Measure on the assumed floor and on an iGPU.
4. **Shadow cascade draw count.** Count the draws in the shadow pass by toggling `CastShadow` on the chunk nodes (the viewer already has the option); answers F-04.
5. **Explosion cost.** Time `RocketFx.Explode` with `Stopwatch` (ticks in microseconds) and count live nodes per second during a gunship burst. `dotnet-counters` for allocation rate (`gc-heap-size`, `alloc-rate`). Answers F-02, F-03.
6. **First-use spikes.** Time the first `ShotFx.Spawn`, first `RocketFx.Explode`, first `CharacterView.Make` and first `GunshipOverlay` shader load with `Time.GetTicksUsec`. Log frame time for the first 60 frames after join. Answers F-08. Why not static: shader compilation is driver-dependent.
7. **Clutter node count.** `ObjectNodeCount` after the clutter build, and the clutter build time per chunk from the existing per-chunk log. Answers F-07 and resolves the 1,715 comment.

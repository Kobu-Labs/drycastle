# The driver's client predicts the amphibious transport (the first predicted land vehicle)

Decided for the amphibious transport's rework. Builds on ADR 0006, which left this open: "predicting every vehicle now (the car model moves through `MoveAndSlide` inside `ServerCar` and is not a pure step; worth doing, but not as a side effect of this)". **Changes** the rule that a vehicle on wheels is simulated by the server only and drawn on every client, the driver's included, about 100 ms in the past. It changes it for this one vehicle; every other car, the boat, the bus and the flak truck stay as they were.

**What changes.** The amphibious transport no longer drives through `CarModel` and `ServerCar.Step`. Its driving is a second pure model in ShooterCore (`DriveModel`, vehicles.json `drive`), stepped by two parties exactly as the helicopter's flight is: the server, which owns the true state, once for each of the driver's inputs, and the driver's client (`DrivePrediction`), which steps its own copy with each input as it sends it and reconciles on the snapshot's acknowledgement. Everyone else, the two riders included, draws it interpolated.

**Why a second model and not a pure `CarModel`.** The planar part of `CarModel` is already pure; what is not is the move, which is Godot's `MoveAndSlide` on the server's vehicle body (floor snapping, slopes, gravity, sliding). Making that pure for every car means replacing how every car collides with the world, and re-tuning them all. That was the line not to cross. A vehicle that needed new handling anyway (weight on land, a boat on water, the two transitions) could bring its own move without touching the others: a hull sweep and ground rays behind one small interface, which is the shape ADR 0006 already proved.

**What it takes.**
- The world is asked the same questions on both sides through one interface (`IDriveWorld`): the ground under a wheel, the water surface, and a sweep of the hull box. In the game that is `DriveProbe` over the solid colliders, as `FlightProbe` is for the helicopter. The hull box is swept half a metre clear of the ground it stands on and lies on the slope under the wheels, so walls, trunks and buildings stop it while kerbs, banks and the slope itself are the wheels' business (four ground rays a step).
- The snapshot gains a drive record per driven vehicle (entity type 6: the speeds along and across the heading and vertically, the slope it stands on, the steering, the throttle and the flags afloat, airborne, climbing out) beside its vehicle record. Protocol 22. What is not sent (the yaw rate) or sent only coarsely (the key ramps) the client keeps from its own prediction.
- The model reads only the quantised `NetInput`, the type's numbers in `vehicles.json`, and the world.

**Considered.** Leaving it interpolated and only improving the numbers (the fallback, had the move needed the rewrite; it would have left the 100 ms plus round trip in the steering, which is what makes a car feel late). Making `CarModel`'s move pure for all cars (the rewrite, above). Putting the new handling into `CarModel` behind flags (it would have tied every car to the transport's tuning).

**Consequences.**
- Two driving models exist. A later car can move onto `DriveModel` by data (a `drive` block), one type at a time; validation limits the block to an amphibious type until someone has driven another on it.
- It floats wherever it is inside a water body and the water over the ground is at least `float_depth` (0.12 m) deep. Depth cannot decide more than that: the village's ponds are 0.2 to 0.9 m deep in the terrain data, so a real draft would never float. The hull is drawn `draft` (0.56 m) under the surface, and in a shallow pond its wheels are under the bed.
- The world's answers differ from `MoveAndSlide`'s in small ways: a ledge higher than `step_height` under one wheel is a wall, a drop deeper than `snap_down` puts it in the air, and it lies on the slope under its wheels (the seats and the ring gun's arc tilt with it; the hit box stays upright, as the helicopter's does).
- A driver whose inputs stop arriving is driven hands off after 6 ticks, so a stalled client's vehicle coasts instead of freezing.
- The driver sees the vehicle in the present and its riders see it about 100 ms in the past. The riders' shots are resolved by the server from its own state, as every shot is.

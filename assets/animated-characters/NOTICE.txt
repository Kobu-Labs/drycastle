soldier-male.glb, soldier-female.glb, rifleman-male.glb and operator.glb come from a "glTF-...-001.zip" archive of 50 low-poly
animated characters (one shared 23-bone "CharacterArmature" rig, 17 clips) that the project owner downloaded and provided as a
local file. They are the archive's Soldier_Male, Soldier_Female, BlueSoldier_Male and Ninja_Sand models, repacked one-to-one from
glTF (embedded buffer) to GLB with no change to geometry, materials or animation. The archive has no licence file and no source
page was recorded; the style matches the Quaternius character packs but that is NOT confirmed, and the files are NOT claimed to
be CC0. Before any release or public distribution the owner must confirm the author, licence and required attribution, then
replace this notice with the real licence text (or remove the files).

The models are flat-colour (no textures, no UVs): materials Skin, Face, Main, Black, DarkGreen, Grey, Details, Helmet, Hair.

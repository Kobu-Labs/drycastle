Weather sounds of the thunderstorm (Weather, #230): rain loops, thunder, an electric crackle.
All cut from free recordings by scripts/sounds/make_weather_recordings.py (trimmed, filtered, faded, level-matched, converted to Ogg Vorbis). Downloaded 2026-11-28 with plain curl.
Every source is CC0 or public domain: no attribution is required (the authors are named anyway).

The twelve models here (alpaca, bull, cow, deer, donkey, fox, horse, husky, shiba-inu, stag, white-horse, wolf) come from
"Animated Animal Pack-glb.zip", which the project owner downloaded and provided as a local file on 2026-11-12. The archive
holds the twelve GLBs only: no licence file, no author, no source page was recorded. They are NOT claimed to be CC0. Before
any release or public distribution the owner must confirm the author, licence and required attribution, then replace this
notice with the real licence text (or remove the files).

Files are the archive's, copied unchanged and renamed to lower case with hyphens (Shiba Inu.glb -> shiba-inu.glb). Each has
one skinned mesh and one skeleton ("AnimalArmature") and the same clips, every one twice (plain and "AnimalArmature|" prefixed;
filter one set when importing): Idle, Idle_2, Idle_Headlow (Idle_2_HeadLow on the dogs, fox, wolf), Eating, Walk, Gallop,
Gallop_Jump, Jump_toIdle, Idle_HitReact_Left, Idle_HitReact_Right, Death, and Attack (dogs, fox, wolf) or Attack_Headbutt and
Attack_Kick (hooved animals). The stag has two meshes (body and antlers).

Suchohrad Game
Goal

A GTA-like open-world game set in the real village of Suchohrad (Slovakia). Realistic building layout mapped to reality, rendered in a simplified low-poly style. Driving and walking, NPCs, a few simple missions.
Hard requirements

    The map must be generated by an automated, re-runnable AI/script pipeline. No manual map building.
    Low-poly stylized look; consistency of style matters more than accuracy.
    Every pipeline step is scripted so fixing a rule and regenerating takes minutes.

Planned stack

    Data: OpenStreetMap (buildings, roads, land use) via Overpass API.
    Terrain: Slovak DMR 3.5 (CC-BY 4.0, GKU Bratislava, via geoportal / ZBGIS). Orthophotos for ground textures (cut out the village area; full files are 100+ GB).
    3D generation: Blender with Blosm (blender-osm) and Buildify (free, Geometry Nodes), or a custom Blender Python script.
    Landmarks (church, pub, etc.): image-to-3D (TRELLIS.2, MIT licence; or Meshy/Tripo free credits) from my own iPhone photos or scans, then cleaned and low-poly-fied. Check Hunyuan3D licence before commercial use.
    Facade colours/heights: Mapillary street imagery (CC BY-SA, API) if coverage exists in Suchohrad; otherwise my own iPhone photos with a vision model.
    Engine: Godot 4. Road Generator add-on (MIT), vehicle physics (Godot Easy Vehicle Physics, MIT, or VitaVehicle).
    Attribution required: (c) OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL); DMR data CC-BY 4.0 GKU.

Pipeline

    Fetch OSM data for Suchohrad and check how complete buildings/roads are (missing buildings, missing height/levels tags).
    Fetch terrain (DMR 3.5) and ortho imagery for the area.
    Blender script: extrude footprints to low-poly buildings, choose roofs/heights/colours from tags and rules, build roads, curbs, fences, trees, terrain.
    Landmarks: image-to-3D, cleanup, decimate, replace generic buildings.
    Export glTF/FBX plus metadata; Godot import script adds colliders, navmesh, road splines for traffic.
    Gameplay: character, car, simple NPCs on the OSM path graph, 1-2 missions. Keep NPCs fictional (no real names or addresses).

Plan

    Prove the pipeline on ONE street first, then scale to the whole village.
    Do not scope-creep: driving plus one mission before anything else.

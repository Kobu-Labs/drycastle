killstreaks will closely follow modern warfare 3 killstraks
in given loadout you can have 3 selected
each requires increasing amount of kills
resets on death

uav
- 4 kills
- shows enemies on minimap for 1min
attack missle
- 5 kills
- a POV missle is spawned high above the user (450 m) and the user controls it, but only direction; it can only dive (between -32 and -80 degrees, never level or straight down), so it hits the ground after about 7 to 13 seconds
turret
- placeable turret that auto aims at enemies
- can be destroyed by 3 meele attacks, or 5 person worth of damage
- 6 kills
care package
- 6 kills
- opening the crate (hold E) gives a random killstreak, not a perk; the crate is gone once opened
- weights (killstreaks.json streak_weights, out of 97): uav 30, attack missile 22, turret 18, helicopter helper 10,
  attack helicopter 9, ac-130 6, moab 2; better streaks are more rare; never another care package
- the reward goes into a held slot at once and does not count toward the kill counter; three reward slots, the oldest
  is replaced when they are full
attack helicopter
- 10 kills
- a helicopter starts circling around the map, user controls the turret and can fire it, POV
helicopter helper
- 15 kills
- a helicopter starts circling around the map and sporadically, yet efficiently shoots at enemies, user does not control it
ac-130
- 18 kills
- an ac-130 starts circling around the map, user controls the turret and can fire it, POV
- unlimited ammo: the streak's time is the only limit (no ammo count, no overheating); the fire rate is the pacing
- 25 mm (fastest), 40 mm and 105 mm (slow shell); every round has a blast, so a near miss still hurts
- rounds reach anywhere on the map (3000 m range); rockets fly at 250 m/s, so a rocket reaches a target across the map
- enemy players and target dummies show a red diamond on the screen while the turret is in use
moab
- 24 kills
- nuclear bomb, kills everyone on map
